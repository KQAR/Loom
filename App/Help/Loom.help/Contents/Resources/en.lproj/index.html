Loom Help

Loom is an HTTP/HTTPS debugging proxy that lives in your menu bar. It captures traffic the way Charles or Proxyman do — and it can also be operated by an AI agent, which is the part that makes it different. You supervise from the console; the agent replays, mocks and diffs.

Start here

  1. Turn the proxy on with the switch at the top right of the menu-bar console.
  2. Point something at it — get traffic in.
  3. If you need to read HTTPS bodies, name the hosts to decrypt. Loom decrypts nothing until you do.
  4. Watch it land in the main window (click the Loom wordmark at the bottom of the console).

Topics

  • Getting traffic into Loom System proxy, curl -x, a phone on the same Wi-Fi, SOCKS5, and reverse-proxy endpoints for clients that ignore proxy settings.
  • Decrypting HTTPS Trusting the certificate, and why the scope is a whitelist you add hosts to.
  • Reading a capture The sidebar, the request table, the inspector, and how filters combine.
  • Changing traffic Replay with overrides, rules that mock or map or block, and breakpoints that hold a request mid-flight.
  • Handing it to an agent Connecting Claude Code or Cursor, what the agent can do, and how you stay in the loop.
  • The menu-bar console What each switch, row and glyph means.
  • When nothing shows up The five reasons a request you expected isn't in the table.
